# Perqz

**Answer honestly. Build better habits. Get rewarded for both.**

[perqz.in](https://perqz.in)

</div>

---

Perqz is a savings-and-self-discovery platform for India, built around three
things that work together:

- **Perqzy** — an AI companion for reflection, habits, and gentle nudges,
  not a therapist and never pretends to be one.
- **A real rewards economy** — points earned through genuine engagement,
  redeemable for gold, travel, insurance, and real brand vouchers, not
  vanity badges.
- **Perqzi** — a physical companion robot, the hardware embodiment of the
  same companion, with its own AI, payments, and third-party integration
  layer.

On the B2B side, **Perqz for Brands** lets companies enroll, publish
public deals and SmartBuy-style vouchers, and reach a real, engaged
consumer base — the same infrastructure that powers the consumer product.
